# Experiment history

| Period / run family | Question and treatment | Evidence and status |
|---|---|---|
| Original taxi and casino prototype | Can a vehicle link work, rest, credit and voluntary gambling? Later variants added debt pressure and recorded risky episodes. | [Original program and media](../../baseline) and [Baseline checksums](../../baseline_manifest.json). Historical mechanics differ from the current economy. |
| Resource environment and navigation calibration | Separate car and personal debt. Add food and fuel, parking, longer service execution and collision verification. Compare shared driver forecasts and scripted feasible routes. | [Controller benchmark](data/diagnostics/navigation_h24_hold4.json) and [Environment checks](data/diagnostics/environment_verification.json). These checks establish mechanics and some feasible paths. |
| Earlier registry and generalist trials | Compare horizons, observations, reward choices and navigation under earlier budgets and transition schemas. | [125-run design](../../EXPERIMENTS.md), [Historical registry](../../experiments/registry.csv) and [Historical results](../historical_results.md). The earlier 18/125 snapshot belongs to this register. |
| $500 debt, public food menu and exploration | Separate simulator execution failures from missing food choice. Add public prices and portion information, target/action checks and short synthetic and real-transition probes. | [Public food menu](../public_menu.md), [Early diagnosis](../training_diagnosis.md), [Calibration catalog](data/diagnostics/calibration_catalog.json) and [Evaluation records](data/local_evaluations.csv). Short food probes establish feasibility in their reset contexts. |
| Completed ownership cohort | Compare DSAC MLP, DSAC GRU, PPO MLP and PPO GRU on a common 14-lesson reset curriculum, one seed and one decision ceiling. | [Frozen design](data/cohort/freeze.json) and [Run outcome](data/cohort/status.json). DSAC GRU passed. The other three stopped at lesson caps. |
| Matched retention and action diagnosis | Replay earlier and later policies in the same food context. Inspect food choice, cash, delays and actor–critic disagreements. | [Retention results](data/diagnostics/food_retention.json), [Per-decision data](data/diagnostics/decisions.csv) and [Transfer analysis](transfer.md). Both tested PPO checkpoints retain the original food-approach skill. |
| Local continuation trials | Change one candidate at a time where possible: curriculum contexts, DSAC categorical temperature, head rescaling and forward-KL actor projection. | [All completed local forks](data/recovery_trials/), [Fixed-input gradient check](data/diagnostics/actor_projection_check.json) and [Pipeline review](../local_food_recovery/PIPELINE_REVIEW.md). Forward KL recovered a narrow food skill, but the full campaign remained 0/16. |
| Tuned recovery and stress tests | Add input history, native PPO exploration, KL guards, standard-reset rehearsal, and stricter qualification. Test the stack, deterministic replay and short full-campaign learning. | [Planned tuned setup](../local_food_recovery/TUNED_COHORT.md) and [Results of the targeted checks](data/smoke/). |
| Frozen S01–S30 suite | Four policies × 30 conditions × 256 episodes. Conditions vary horizon, resources, debt, casino availability, controls and food. Stress banks carry paired controls. | [Condition register](data/frozen_conditions.csv) and [Original scenario specification](data/diagnostics/debt500_scenarios.json). The selected DSAC GRU was evaluated on all 30 conditions. [Final evaluation](final_dsac_gru.md). |

The [result record](results.md) keeps the completed ownership cohort and the standalone final evaluation separate. Earlier report counts, warm forks and repeated evaluations serve different purposes and are kept separately. The matched visualizations use the first 16 standard development IDs in all four panels.

See [problems and fixes](problems_and_fixes.md) for the diagnostic evidence and scope of each correction.
